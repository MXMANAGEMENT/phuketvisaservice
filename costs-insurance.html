How Direct Billing Works

  1. Contact us before your appointment with your insurance details.
  2. We verify your coverage and obtain a guarantee of payment if required.
  3. Receive treatment without paying upfront (for covered services).
  4. If direct billing isn't possible, we provide a detailed English/German invoice for your reimbursement claim.

Frequently Asked Questions

No, you can book directly with us. However, check with your insurance provider if they require a referral for reimbursement purposes.

Can I pay with Credit Card?

Yes, we accept Visa, MasterCard, and Thai QR Payment. Cash (THB) is also welcome.

Do you issue tax invoices (VAT)?

Yes, all our invoices are official tax invoices valid for insurance claims worldwide.
