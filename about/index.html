About Visa Service Phuket

We are a small, local visa support office in Wichit, Phuket. We help expats, retirees, remote workers and visitors deal with Thai Immigration paperwork, clearly, honestly and without unnecessary upselling.

What we do

We prepare your documents, check your TM30, explain what Immigration expects and guide you through the appointment. We don't make immigration decisions, the officers do that. What we do is make sure you walk in prepared and confident, with nothing missing.

Our services include visa extensions, retirement visa support, Non-Immigrant visa guidance, DTV visa help, 90-day reports, re-entry permits, TM30 checks and Thai driving license conversion.

Why people work with us

  • We are here. We live and work in Phuket. We know Phuket Immigration, the DLT, and the local process first-hand.
  • We speak your language. English, German and Russian, no translation apps, no miscommunication.
  • We are honest. If something won't work for your situation, we say so upfront. No surprises, no hidden steps.
  • We answer fast. WhatsApp is our main channel. Most messages get a reply within minutes during office hours.
  • We focus on preparation. The better your documents are prepared, the smoother it goes. That's our entire approach.

How it works

  1. You message us. Tell us your nationality, visa type and what you need. WhatsApp is easiest.
  2. We check your situation. We look at your case and tell you what's realistic and what to expect.
  3. We prepare your paperwork. You get a clear document list. We check everything before you go.
  4. You attend prepared. No missing papers, no wasted trips, no confusion at the counter.

Office

Our office is in Wichit, Mueang Phuket, close to Phuket Immigration. We meet clients by appointment.
