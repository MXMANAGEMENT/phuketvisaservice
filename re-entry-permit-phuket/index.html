Re-Entry Permit in Phuket

If you have a Thai extension of stay and you leave the country without a re-entry permit, your permission to stay is cancelled. A re-entry permit protects it — we help you get the right one before you fly.

Phuket Insider Tip
✈️
Airport service is limited Phuket International Airport does have a Re-Entry Permit desk, but it's only available during specific hours and can be busy. For early morning or late-night flights, we strongly recommend getting your permit at the main Immigration office in Wichit first.

A re-entry permit in Thailand preserves your visa extension when you leave the country. Without one, your extension is automatically cancelled — even for a short trip. We arrange it quickly before your departure.

Why you need one

Your one-year extension (for example retirement or marriage) is tied to staying in Thailand. Leave without a re-entry permit and it is voided — you would have to start again. A re-entry permit keeps it valid while you travel.

Single vs multiple

  • Single re-entry — for one trip out and back
  • Multiple re-entry — for several trips during your permitted stay

You can get one at Phuket Immigration or, at short notice, at the airport before departure.

How we help

We tell you which permit fits your travel plans, prepare the form (TM8) and photo, and make sure it is done before you leave so your stay stays intact.

⚠️ Leaving Thailand without a re-entry permit cancels your entire extension of stay — even for a weekend trip. Getting one takes about an hour.

Common questions

Do I need a re-entry permit for a short trip?
Yes — any departure without one cancels your extension of stay, even for a weekend trip. A single re-entry permit covers one round trip.
Can I get a re-entry permit at Phuket airport?
Often yes, at the Immigration desk before departure, but queues can be tight. Getting it in advance at Phuket Immigration is calmer — we help with both.
Single or multiple re-entry permit — which is better?
If you travel once, single is enough. If you fly several times before your stay ends, a multiple permit is usually more convenient.
Can I get a re-entry permit at the airport?
Yes, re-entry permits are available at Phuket International Airport, but only during certain hours and with limited staff. Getting one before your trip is safer — we arrange it quickly.
What is the difference between single and multiple re-entry permits?
A single re-entry permit covers one departure and return. A multiple permit covers unlimited trips for the duration of your visa extension. We advise which one fits your travel plans.
