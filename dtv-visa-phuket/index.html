DTV Visa Guidance in Phuket

The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is a long-validity visa aimed at remote workers, freelancers and people joining Thai soft-power activities. If you are exploring it from Phuket, we help you understand the basics and prepare.

The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is designed for remote workers, digital nomads and long-stay visitors. It allows stays of up to 180 days, extendable once. We check if DTV fits your profile and prepare the application.

Phuket Insider Tip
💻
DTV evidence is getting stricter While the DTV is a great option, Phuket Immigration is increasingly thorough with remote work evidence. We recommend having your last 3 months of invoices or a formal employment contract ready. We'll help you review your portfolio to ensure it meets the current 'Phuket standard'.

Who the DTV suits

The DTV is generally aimed at remote workers and digital nomads working for employers or clients outside Thailand, and at applicants joining qualifying activities such as Muay Thai, cooking courses or medical treatment. It offers a long validity with a defined stay per entry.

What you may need

  • Proof of remote work or the qualifying activity
  • Evidence of funds
  • Passport and supporting documents

DTV rules are still settling and can differ by embassy and over time. We help you understand current expectations rather than guesswork.

How we help

We explain whether the DTV fits your situation versus other options, which documents tend to be expected, and how it interacts with reporting once you are in Phuket. If another visa is a better fit, we say so.

Common questions

What is the DTV visa?
The Destination Thailand Visa is a long-validity Thai visa for remote workers, freelancers and people joining qualifying activities, allowing extended stays per entry.
Can I get the DTV while in Phuket?
The DTV is typically applied for at a Thai embassy or consulate. We help you understand the process and prepare your documents from Phuket.
Is the DTV right for me, or should I use another visa?
It depends on your work, finances and plans. We compare the DTV against options like Non-O or extensions so you choose the right route.
Who qualifies for a DTV visa in Thailand?
The DTV is aimed at remote workers, freelancers, digital nomads and people participating in Thai activities like Muay Thai or cooking courses. We check if your situation qualifies.
Can I extend a DTV visa in Thailand?
Yes, the initial 180-day stay can be extended once for another 180 days at Immigration. We handle the extension paperwork when the time comes.
