Phuket Immigration Office. Visitor Guide

Phuket Immigration (in Tambon Wichit, Mueang Phuket) handles visa extensions, 90-day reports, re-entry permits, TM30 and more. Going prepared saves hours, here is what to know, and how we can get you ready.

Phuket Insider Tip
🗺️
Two offices, one main hub Phuket Immigration has two locations. While there is a small branch in Patong for some services, the *main* hub for almost all visa extensions and complex services is the office in Wichit (near Phuket Town). Check with us before you drive to the wrong one!

What Phuket Immigration handles

  • Visa extensions (tourist, retirement, marriage and more)
  • 90-day address reports
  • Re-entry permits
  • TM30 address notifications
  • Certificates of residence

What to bring (general)

Bring your passport, copies of the photo page, current visa and latest entry stamp (signed), a valid TM30, the relevant form for your task, and any supporting documents (financial proof, marriage or retirement evidence). Requirements differ by task, we give you a precise checklist.

Avoid a wasted trip: the most common reasons people are turned away are a missing TM30, unsigned copies, or incomplete financial evidence.

Tips for a smoother visit

  • Go early, queues build up later in the day
  • Bring signed copies of every key page
  • Check your TM30 is current before you go
  • Carry a backup of your financial documents

We prepare you, or come with you

We review your task, build your exact document list, check your TM30, and can guide you through the visit so it goes smoothly. Send your situation on WhatsApp and we will tell you exactly what to bring.

Common questions

Where is Phuket Immigration located?
The main Phuket Immigration office is in Tambon Wichit, Mueang Phuket District. Send us a message and we will help you plan your visit and what to bring.
What are Phuket Immigration opening hours?
Government offices generally open on weekdays during business hours and close on public holidays. Hours can change, so confirm before you travel, we can check for you.
Can you go to Immigration with me?
We prepare your documents and guide you through the process, and can support you on the day so your visit goes smoothly.
Why was my application refused at Immigration?
Common reasons are a missing or outdated TM30, unsigned copies, or incomplete evidence. We pre-check everything to avoid this.
