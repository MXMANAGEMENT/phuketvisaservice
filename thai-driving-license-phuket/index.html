Thai Driving License in Phuket

Driving in Phuket is far safer and cheaper with a Thai license — and many foreign licenses can be converted at the Department of Land Transport (DLT). We explain the documents and prepare you for the visit.

Getting a Thai driving license in Phuket involves converting your foreign license at the Department of Land Transport (DLT) in Kathu. We prepare documents, book the appointment and guide you through the process — usually done in one day.

Phuket Insider Tip
🚗
DLT appointments book out fast The Department of Land Transport (DLT) in Kathu/Phuket often has a long waiting list. If you need your license quickly, contact us — we can often help you find cancellation slots or guide you to less crowded provincial offices nearby.

Who can convert a license

If you hold a valid foreign driving license or an International Driving Permit, you can usually obtain a Thai license at the Phuket DLT. Without a recognised license you may need to take the practical test as well.

Documents you usually need

  • Passport with a valid visa and entry stamp
  • Proof of address (residence certificate from Immigration or your embassy)
  • Recent medical certificate
  • Your foreign license or IDP, sometimes with a translation

Note: even converted licenses usually require the short video plus colour, reflex and depth-perception checks at the DLT.

How we help

We give you the exact checklist, help you obtain the residence certificate and medical certificate, explain the DLT process and tests, and get you ready so the visit goes smoothly.

Common questions

Can I convert my foreign driving license in Phuket?
Often yes. With a valid foreign license or IDP plus the right documents, you can usually obtain a Thai license at the Phuket DLT without the full driving test.
Do I need a residence certificate for a Thai driving license?
Usually yes — proof of address from Phuket Immigration or your embassy is commonly required. We help you get it.
Do I need to take a test?
If your foreign license is recognised, you typically skip the practical test but still complete the short physical checks (reflex, colour, depth). Without a recognised license, a test may apply.
How long is a first Thai driving license valid?
A first Thai license is usually issued as a two-year license, then renewable for longer periods. The DLT confirms the exact validity on the day.
Do I need a medical certificate for a Thai driving license?
Yes, a medical certificate from a Thai clinic is required. It is a simple checkup that takes about 15 minutes. We can recommend a nearby clinic.
How long is a Thai driving license valid?
The first license is typically valid for 2 years, then renewable for 5 years at a time. We help with first-time conversion and renewals.
