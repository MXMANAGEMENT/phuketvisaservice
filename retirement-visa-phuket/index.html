Retirement Visa in Phuket

If you are 50 or older and want to settle in Phuket, a retirement-based stay (Non-Immigrant O or O-A, then a one-year extension) is the usual route. We make the requirements clear and get your paperwork ready.

A retirement visa in Phuket is available for foreigners aged 50 and over, based on a Non-Immigrant O or O-A visa. We check your eligibility, prepare documents and guide you through the process, reach out for honest advice.

Phuket Insider Tip
🏦
Same-day bank updates are critical Phuket Immigration is very strict about bank book updates. Your bank book must be updated on the *very same day* you submit your application. Even a 24-hour gap can lead to a rejection. We'll coordinate the timing with you perfectly.

Who it's for

Retirement-based stays are for foreigners aged 50+ who want to live in Thailand long-term without working. Most people enter on a Non-O or O-A visa and then apply for a one-year extension of stay at Phuket Immigration.

What you generally need

  • Passport and the correct entry visa (Non-O / O-A)
  • Proof of the required funds (Thai bank deposit, monthly income, or a combination)
  • TM30 address notification
  • Supporting forms and photos for the extension

Financial rules vary and are updated from time to time. We confirm the current amounts and how to season your funds before you apply.

How we help

We check whether you meet the conditions, plan the timeline (visa first, extension later), prepare your bank and document evidence, and guide you through the Phuket Immigration appointment.

After your retirement extension

Once approved, you stay compliant with a 90-day report and, if you travel, a re-entry permit. We can help with all of it so your long stay stays clean.

Common questions

How old do I need to be for a retirement visa in Thailand?
The retirement route is for applicants aged 50 and over. Younger applicants usually need a different visa type, we can advise which fits.
How much money do I need for a retirement visa?
There is a financial requirement met by a Thai bank balance, monthly income, or a combination. The exact figures and seasoning periods change over time, so we confirm the current rules for your case.
Can you help with the retirement extension in Phuket?
Yes. We help with the documents and guide your appointment at Phuket Immigration for the one-year extension of stay.
Do I need health insurance for a retirement visa?
Some retirement routes (especially O-A) can require qualifying health insurance. We tell you whether it applies to your situation.
What are the financial requirements for a Thai retirement visa?
Financial requirements depend on whether you apply for a Non-O or O-A visa and your home country. Contact us with your details and we explain exactly what you need.
Can I work on a retirement visa in Thailand?
No. A retirement visa does not include a work permit. If you need to work, a different visa category applies, we can advise you.
