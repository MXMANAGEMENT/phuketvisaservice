Common questions about visas in Phuket

Can I extend my visa in Phuket?
In many cases, yes. A range of visas can be extended at Phuket Immigration, depending on your visa type and personal situation. We review your case, explain which option fits and help you prepare the documents before your appointment.
Do I need a TM30 for visa support?
A valid TM30 (notification of residence) is often required for visa-related applications in Thailand. We check whether your TM30 is in order and guide you on how to update it if needed.
Can you help with retirement visa support in Phuket?
Yes. We support applicants who want to stay in Thailand on a retirement basis by explaining the requirements, helping you organise the paperwork and guiding you through the appointment step by step.
Can I convert my foreign driving license in Thailand?
Often yes. Many foreign licenses can be used to obtain a Thai driving license through the Department of Land Transport (DLT). We explain the documents you need and help you prepare for the process in Phuket.
How long does the process take?
It depends on the service, your documents and current processing times at Immigration or the DLT. After reviewing your situation we give you a realistic estimate so there are no surprises.
Do I need to visit your office?
Many questions can be handled on WhatsApp first. Depending on your service, some steps require you to attend in person. We tell you clearly in advance what needs to be done where.
Can you help if my visa expires soon?
Yes, the sooner you contact us, the more options you usually have. Send us your visa type and expiry date on WhatsApp and we will tell you the next step quickly.
Do you guarantee visa approval?
No service provider can guarantee immigration approval. We help you understand the requirements, prepare documents correctly and guide you through the process based on your situation.
